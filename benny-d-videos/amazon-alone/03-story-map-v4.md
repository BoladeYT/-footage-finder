## THE STORY IN BRIEF
At dusk, on a short trail behind a river lodge in the central Amazon, a crashing in the undergrowth sends "you" running from a jaguar you never see. When you stop, your shin is cut open and the trail is gone. Over three days, you do what most people would do: you try to walk back in a straight line, marking your way as you go, until you find your own marks and realise you've been walking in circles. The forest never attacks you; it wears you down through a cut that turns hot and swollen, feet that never dry, and water you can't trust. A new rule that doesn't depend on your senses (go downhill, find water, follow it) gets you to the open riverbank where a lodge boat sees you. Then you learn how close you were the whole time.
