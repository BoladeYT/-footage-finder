# STORY DIRECTIONS: Amazon Alone
Status: Offered to the creator before the full Story Map (the ending's direction changes most of the map)

Common to all directions (from the card and brief):
- "You" are Benny D's figure, an adult visitor staying at a small lodge on a river in the central Amazon, in the wet season (F21). People at the lodge will notice you're gone, so a search exists (F10).
- A noise in the trees at dusk triggers fear of a jaguar. You hurry off the path, and in that panic you get a cut on your shin and lose the trail (fear doing the damage, F01–F02).
- The small things carry the story: the cut (F12, F13), wet feet that never dry (F11, the clock starts at about 48 h), water you can't read (F14–F17), and a canopy that hides the sun (F08, F09).

## Direction 1: Stay and be found
- Shape: two nights mostly in one place. The pressure is staying put while every instinct says move.
- Emotional core: holding your nerve. Doing the unglamorous right things (staying put, signalling, keeping the cut clean, getting water) while the body slowly frays.
- Ending direction: searchers find you because of choices you made (where you stayed, how you signalled). Survival with a cost.
- Technique: none beyond clear cause and effect.
- Rough length: about 9–11 minutes.
- Risk: less movement, so the story leans on endurance and texture.

## Direction 2: Walk, circle, rethink (recommended)
- Shape: about three days of compounding choices. You do what most viewers would do and walk to get out. At the midpoint you find your own trail: you've been walking in circles (F07, F09). You rethink, and change strategy.
- Emotional core: the moment your own senses are proven wrong, then earning your way back with better judgment while the cut and your feet get worse.
- Ending direction: survival with a credible cost. Your changed strategy decides it: you stop wandering, make yourself findable, and follow water to the river the lodge sits on (F10). You come out with damaged feet and an infected cut, and some risks may outlast the story (F19, F20).
- Technique: one fair discovery (crossing your own track), set up early by a mark you leave.
- Rough length: about 11–14 minutes.
- Risk: the river ending must come from your decision, never from luck.

## Optional sting (works with either): "So close"
- At the end you learn you were never far from the lodge. It reframes the whole ordeal: the jungle didn't trap you; your senses did.
- Fair-play basis: established early (you only left the path for a few minutes) and explained by F07 and F09.
- Note: how far people actually get from camp is unverified (X06), so the distance would be this story's fiction, never claimed as typical.
